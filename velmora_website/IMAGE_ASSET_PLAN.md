# VELMORA image asset plan

## How we can expand the image library

1. **Create a consistent campaign set**: generate or commission original fashion images in groups (hero campaign, women, men, accessories, fabric/detail, editorial, product studio). Keep the same lighting, color grade, and luxury editorial direction.
2. **Use real product photography for products being sold**: do not use AI concept images as if they are accurate photos of stock. For a demo portfolio, label concept imagery clearly; before real checkout, replace it with the exact product, color, fit, and details.
3. **Keep assets local**: save approved files under `assets/`, use descriptive names (for example `campaign-womens-tailoring-01.webp`), and update the relevant HTML/CSS image references. This avoids hotlink breakage and gives the site predictable loading.
4. **Optimize each file**: use WebP or AVIF for photos, target roughly 200–450 KB for large campaign images and 80–180 KB for product thumbnails when quality allows; keep the logo as SVG/transparent PNG. Provide width/height or aspect-ratio styling to reduce layout shifts.
5. **Use responsive crops**: hero imagery should have desktop and mobile crops where possible. Keep faces, garments, and bags inside the focal safe area.
6. **Track licensing**: use original generated images, owned photography, or assets with clear commercial-use permission. Keep a small asset log noting source, license, and intended placement.
